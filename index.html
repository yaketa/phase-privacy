Privacy Policy — Phase

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Summary. Phase does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no servers, and no networking code, and it never sends anything anywhere. What you write leaves this iPhone only through iOS features you control, such as your device backup (see section 2).

1. Who is responsible

Phase is made by yaketa, an independent developer. Contact: phase.app.support@gmail.com.

2. What Phase stores, and where

Phase stores the following on your device, and nothing else:

  • The time slots you create — label, start time, end time, and whether that slot sends a notification.
  • The memo text you write for each slot.
  • Any photos you attach, which are copied into Phase's own private storage inside the app. Phase makes that copy by re-encoding the picture itself, so the location and camera information the original carried — GPS coordinates, the date and time it was taken, the device and lens — does not come with it.
  • One preference that controls whether notifications show your memo text.

This information is written to your device's storage, and Phase never transmits any of it. If you back your iPhone up to iCloud or to a computer, your Phase data is included in that backup like any other app's data; you can exclude Phase from iCloud Backup in iOS Settings.

Phase does not read your photo library — photos are chosen through Apple's system photo picker, which runs outside the app and hands Phase only the images you pick.

3. What Phase does not do

Phase does not collect, transmit, upload, or sync your data, and it keeps no backup of its own — the device backup described in section 2 is an iOS feature, not Phase's. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDK, no third-party SDKs, no tracking of any kind, and no account or sign-in. It contains no networking code and works with no network connection at all.

4. Sharing with third parties

Phase shares no user data with anyone — nothing it stores ever reaches us, and it has no means to share it. Two things are worth stating plainly: downloading Phase is a transaction with Apple and is covered by Apple's own privacy policy; and this policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, so opening it makes an ordinary web request that GitHub may log (for example your IP address). That is this website, not the app.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Phase asks iOS to show a local notification at the start of each time slot you have switched notifications on for. These are scheduled by iOS on your device. No push server is involved, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. By default a notification shows the slot's label and the beginning of your memo, which means memo text can appear on the lock screen; choose "Label only" in Settings and notifications will show only the label and the time. You can withdraw notification permission at any time in iOS Settings › Notifications › Phase.

6. How long your data is kept, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. We keep nothing, so there is nothing for us to retain or erase.

  • Delete one photo: Settings › the time slot › tap ✕ on the thumbnail.
  • Delete one time slot and its photos: Settings › the time slot › Delete This Slot.
  • Delete the slots you added or edited and restore the original four: Settings › Reset to Defaults.
  • Delete everything on this device: touch and hold the Phase icon and choose "Delete App". iOS deletes the app's entire storage, including attached images, and cancels any notifications it had scheduled.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because Phase never sends us any data, we hold nothing from the app and there is nothing for us to act on. The one exception is email: if you write to us, we see your address and whatever your message contains; we use it only to reply, and we delete the exchange once the matter is closed. Write to phase.app.support@gmail.com for any of the above and we will respond within 30 days. If you need a postal address for a formal request, ask by email and we will provide it. We do not sell or share your personal information.

8. Children

Phase is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/EEA). It collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

9. Security

Your data lives in Phase's private app container and is protected by the device-level protections iOS applies to app data. Those protections depend on your having a passcode set on this iPhone. Phase adds no encryption of its own, and it has no server-side component to secure.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact: phase.app.support@gmail.com
