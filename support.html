The following makes a bug far quicker to fix:

  • Your iOS version and iPhone model (Settings › General › About)
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

Questions people ask

A notification didn't arrive. There are two switches, and both have to be on. First, iOS Settings › Notifications › Phase must allow notifications. Second, each time slot has its own notification toggle on its detail screen (Settings › the time slot). A slot you added yourself starts with its notification switched off.

Two time slots overlap. Which one shows? The one you added first. The list in Settings is sorted by start time for readability, but the slot that wins is the earlier one in the order you created them. Overlapping slots are marked with an "Overlap" badge so you can find them.
